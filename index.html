About Us

Focused on strong basics, not just marks

Bright Future Coaching Classes, located in Tulsi Vihar Colony, 24 Futa Road, Dadri (near Shiv Mandir), teaches Maths, Science and English to students from Class 5 to Class 10.

Our focus is on concept clarity — completing the syllabus on time and conducting 1–2 full revisions before exams, so students can attempt their papers with confidence.

FAQ

What are the fees?

Fees vary by batch and class. Please call or WhatsApp us for exact details, and we'll share the complete fee structure with you.

Is the demo class free?

Yes, the first demo class is completely free. You can see our teaching style and classroom environment before enrolling.

What are the batch timings?

Batches run Monday to Saturday, between 4:00 PM and 8:00 PM. Sunday is reserved for a doubt-clearing session.

Are study notes provided?

Yes, study material and practice sheets for every subject are included — at no extra charge.
